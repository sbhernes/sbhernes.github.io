**Why VMware Explorer Las Vegas Should Be on Your Radar This Year**

Posted 21.06 2025

In this article, I’ll share why I believe it’s a must-attend event based on my own experience from the past years. I’ll also walk you through the top sessions I’m most excited about this year, especially those that tackle real-world challenges like VCF implementation, migration, and automation.

## Why You Should Attend Explorer Las Vegas *AUGUST 25 – 28*

Explorer Las Vegas is just around the corner, and if you work with VMware technology or just want to stay ahead in the cloud game this is the event you don’t want to miss.

### Why Attend?

#### 💡 Get Fresh Ideas and Real-World Solutions

This conference is the perfect opportunity to learn how others have solved challenges similar to the ones you’re facing today. You'll hear real-world customer stories, lessons learned, and creative solutions that can inspire your next big move.

#### 🚀 Stay Updated with the Latest Tech

Get a serious dose of knowledge refresh! on the newest technologies. Whether you're looking to implement VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF), migrate workloads, or optimize private cloud strategy, this event will help you align your organization's tech direction with the future.

#### 🤝 Meet Other Enthusiasts

You’ll connect with people who are just as passionate about cloud and virtualization as you are. Whether it's during a breakout session, a roundtable, or over a coffee, there’s no better place to grow your professional network.

#### 🤗 Team Building

Traveling and attending sessions together with your team is a great bonding experience. You'll learn more about each other’s strengths, share insights, and come back aligned and motivated.

#### 🌟 My Personal Experience

This will be my fourth year in a row attending Explorer, and each year has brought something new. I’ve met incredible people, discovered solutions and upon returning from the conferences, implemented many of the solutions in our own environment. Every year, I return with more motivation and knowledge and closer bonds with the team I travel with.
